How to solve a question

1

Open Decoy AI and tap the upload zone on the home screen

2

Take a photo of your question or choose one from your library

3

Tap Solve — your step-by-step explanation will appear in seconds

4

Tap any step and ask a follow-up question if you need more explanation

How to use Decoy Mode

1

Tap the 🧮 Decoy Mode button on the solver screen

2

The app instantly switches to a fully working calculator

3

To return, enter your secret PIN into the calculator and tap =

Your PIN is set during onboarding. To change it, go to Settings → Security → Change PIN.

How to cancel your subscription

1

Open the Settings app on your iPhone

2

Tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions

3

Tap Decoy AI → Cancel Subscription

Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How to restore purchases

1

Open Decoy AI and go to Settings (gear icon)

2

Tap Restore Purchases under the Account section

3

Sign in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase if prompted
